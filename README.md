Telco Customer Churn Analysis (SQL + Power BI)

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end customer churn analysis for a telecommunications company using **SQL and Power BI**. The objective is to quantify churn impact, identify key churn drivers, and recommend actionable retention strategies through business-focused dashboards.

---

## Business Objective

* Measure customer churn and revenue impact
* Identify high-risk customer segments and churn drivers
* Support data-driven retention decisions

---

## Tools & Technologies

* **SQL:** Data analysis, churn metrics, revenue-at-risk calculations
* **Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation

---

## SQL Usage

SQL was used as the analytical layer to:

* Calculate churn rate and churned customer counts
* Analyze churn patterns by customer type and contract
* Quantify revenue at risk from churned customers
* Validate key Power BI metrics before visualization

All SQL queries are included in the `sql/` folder.

---

## Dashboard Structure

### Page 1 – Executive Summary

* Total customers, churned customers, churn rate
* High-level revenue impact of churn

### Page 2 – Churn Drivers Analysis

* Churn patterns by customer type and contract
* Identification of key drivers behind customer churn

### Page 3 – Retention Strategy & Insights

* Retention Priority Matrix combining churn rate and revenue at risk
* Identification of high-risk customer segments
* Clear retention focus areas

---

## Key Insights

* Single Adult and Senior Individual customers represent the highest churn risk
* Month-to-month contracts are the primary driver of customer churn
* Longer-term contracts significantly reduce churn and stabilize revenue

---

## Business Recommendations

* Prioritize retention efforts for high-risk customer segments
* Encourage migration from month-to-month to longer-term contracts
* Focus retention investment where churn rate and revenue risk overlap
